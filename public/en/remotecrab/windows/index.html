Why the prompts — stated plainly

What "Unknown publisher" means

A code-signing certificate costs money every year and we have not bought one yet. So Windows sees an executable and cannot tell who made it — and says so.

This is not a virus warning. A real one is a red full-screen page with a single button: "Don't run".

Why only one “Yes”

UAC appears because the installer writes to C:\Program Files, which needs administrator rights. Every installer asks.

Unblock the ZIP in step 2, then run install.cmd. You normally see this one prompt only; launching and auto-updating later do not need admin rights.

FAQ

I still got "Windows protected your PC"

That means step 2 was skipped, or you double-clicked the .exe inside the ZIP. "More info" → "Run anyway" works too, but extracting again after unblocking is cleaner.

What does install.cmd do?

Open it in Notepad — it is plain text. It unblocks every file in the folder, runs msiexec silently, and starts RemoteCrab. No network access, no downloads, nothing else.

It will not connect to my phone

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi and the app is open on the phone. The first connection asks for approval on the phone. If your network isolates clients, try a phone hotspot once.

How do I uninstall?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → RemoteCrab → Uninstall. The virtual camera is removed with it.

Why is it not on the Microsoft Store?

It installs a virtual camera and a background service, neither of which can ship inside the Store's sandbox. Direct download is the only way to keep every feature.
